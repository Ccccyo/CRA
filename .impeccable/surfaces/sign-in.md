# Sign-in surface

Mode: Operate. Audience: CRA analysts, reviewers, and administrators entering a demo workbench. Primary task: authenticate with a known account and understand which database-backed role controls the session.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Login is the first control point of the review workflow, not a generic account card. The surface refuses the centered SaaS form floating in empty space.

OWN-WORLD: Cold white and pale cobalt work surfaces, ink-blue text, thin evidence-led dividers, 12px corners, and a single cobalt primary action. Role information reads as a compact access register rather than promotional cards.

STORY: The user identifies the correct demo identity, fills its email with one action, enters the private password, and understands that the database role—not a choice on this page—determines access.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A single split work surface fills the useful desktop width. The left access register explains the controlled workflow and lists administrator, analyst, and reviewer accounts. The right side carries the focused sign-in form. On mobile, the form comes first and the account register follows.

FORM: Code-led Operate surface, shaped directly from the established CRA design system. seed: none (unavailable).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
